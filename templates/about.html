Mission

Why We Built OptiCrop

Agriculture depends largely on the nature of soil and climatic conditions. Unpredictable changes — non-seasonal rainfall, heat waves, humidity fluctuations — cause tremendous losses for farmers who rely on intuition alone.

OptiCrop was built to bridge this gap. By training a Machine Learning model on 2,200 crop samples with 7 key soil and climate features, we help farmers understand exactly which crop will thrive under their specific conditions.
